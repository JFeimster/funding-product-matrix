## Positioning

**Funding Agency Automation Pack** is a practical broker operations system for reducing pipeline chaos, improving lead follow-up, organizing borrower documents, protecting sensitive workflows, and building a cleaner funding agency operating system.

This is **not** a generic AI tool pack. It is built for funding broker workflow automation across lead intake, CRM hygiene, document collection, deal summaries, referral partner follow-up, lender-fit preparation, and human-reviewed money conversations.

## Core Doctrine

> Automate the admin. Assist the analysis. Human-review the money talk. Audit everything.

Use this doctrine as the operating rule for every asset, workflow, prompt, automation, and CRM field in the pack.

## Primary Audiences

- Existing loan brokers

## Broker Safety Note

Not lender, legal, tax, accounting, or underwriting advice. Approval is not guaranteed. Eligibility, terms, documentation requirements, and lender review vary. Human review required.

Do not use this pack to guarantee approval, promise income, imply every borrower qualifies, quote terms without lender review, replace underwriting judgment, or automate compliance-sensitive communication without a human checkpoint.
