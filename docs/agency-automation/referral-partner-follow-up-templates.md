# Referral Partner Follow-Up Templates

## Short Description

This file gives funding brokers, ISOs, referral partners, processors, and funding agency owners a practical follow-up system for managing referral relationships.

Use it to stay visible with accountants, bookkeepers, consultants, ecommerce advisors, real estate professionals, business coaches, existing clients, and local business connectors without sounding like a desperate commission raccoon.

The goal is simple:

**Make referrals easier to send, easier to track, easier to update, and easier to keep alive.**

This is part of the **Funding Agency Automation Pack** by Moonshine Capital.

Core doctrine:

**Automate the admin. Assist the analysis. Human-review the money talk. Audit everything.**

## How to Use This File

Use these templates inside your CRM, email platform, SMS tool, LinkedIn messages, or referral partner follow-up workflow.

Recommended workflow:

1. Pick the partner type.
2. Choose the template based on the relationship stage.
3. Personalize the first line.
4. Add the borrower or referral context when appropriate.
5. Track every touch in your CRM.
6. Set a next follow-up date.
7. Keep sensitive borrower details out of casual messages unless the borrower has authorized sharing.
8. Use human review before sending anything involving funding eligibility, lender feedback, offers, declines, or deal-specific risk notes.

Do not blast these blindly. Templates are leverage, not a license to act like a spam cannon in a blazer.

## Follow-Up Cadence

### New Referral Partner Cadence

| Timing | Touch | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | First outreach | Start the conversation. |
| Day 3 | Light follow-up | Clarify who you help and how referrals work. |
| Day 7 | Value-add message | Share checklist, intake link, or example referral profile. |
| Day 14 | Soft check-in | Ask if they have any clients who may need funding guidance. |
| Day 30 | Partner invite | Invite them to join or explore the partner program. |
| Monthly | Relationship nurture | Stay visible without being annoying. |

### Active Referral Partner Cadence

| Trigger | Touch | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Referral submitted | Confirmation | Acknowledge receipt and next step. |
| Borrower contacted | Status update | Let partner know outreach started. |
| Docs missing | Request help if appropriate | Keep file moving. |
| Submitted to lender | Limited update | Confirm progress without over-disclosing. |
| Offer received | Human-reviewed update | Avoid quoting terms casually. |
| Funded | Thank-you | Reinforce relationship. |
| Not ready | Nurture plan | Keep partner engaged. |
| No referrals in 30 days | Check-in | Reopen the channel. |

## What Not to Say

| Do Not Say | Why It Is Risky | Safer Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| “Everyone qualifies.” | Implies universal eligibility. | “Eligibility varies based on the borrower profile and lender review.” |
| “Guaranteed approval.” | Creates a false funding promise. | “Approval is not guaranteed.” |
| “I can get anyone funded.” | Misleading and reckless. | “I can help business owners explore available options.” |
| “No documents needed.” | Documentation may be required. | “Documentation requirements vary by product and lender.” |
| “Send me their bank logins.” | Unsafe and inappropriate. | “Use the approved secure bank connection or document upload process.” |
| “This lender will approve them.” | Final decision belongs to lender/review process. | “This may be worth reviewing for lender fit.” |
| “They’re definitely getting an offer.” | Promise without review. | “The file can be reviewed once the required information is complete.” |
| “You’ll make easy money referring deals.” | Income claim risk. | “Referral compensation, if available, depends on program rules and completed eligible activity.” |
| “AI handles the whole thing.” | Overstates automation and ignores human review. | “AI may help with admin, summaries, and follow-up drafts, but human review is required.” |
| “Don’t worry about consent.” | Dangerous data handling. | “Borrower authorization should be confirmed before sharing file-specific details.” |

## Broker Safety Note

Referral partner communication can involve sensitive borrower data, funding claims, lender communication, compensation questions, and deal status updates.

Use these guardrails:

- Do not guarantee funding approval.
- Do not promise referral income.
- Do not imply every borrower qualifies.
- Do not claim Moonshine Capital is a bank.
- Do not share borrower-specific details with a referral partner unless borrower authorization is confirmed.
- Do not send bank statements, IDs, tax returns, or sensitive borrower data through unsecured channels.
- Do not quote final terms unless they come from an authorized lender or funding provider and have been reviewed.
- Do not provide legal, tax, accounting, or underwriting advice.
- Use careful language: “may help,” “can support,” “eligibility varies,” “not a guarantee,” “terms may change,” “lender review required,” and “human review required.”
- Keep CRM notes clean, factual, and audit-friendly.

AI can draft messages, summarize partner activity, and create follow-up tasks. It should not independently make funding claims, disclose sensitive borrower data, approve deals, explain final offers, or replace broker judgment.
