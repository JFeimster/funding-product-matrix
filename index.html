QUICK ROUTING REMINDER

Do Not Route by Panic.

Before you pitch a funding product, check the borrower’s use of funds, repayment source, urgency, documents, existing debt, and cash-flow pressure.

  • What is the money actually for?
  • How will repayment fit the business cash flow?
  • Is this urgent, or just underprepared?
  • Are the documents ready?
  • Does the borrower need funding now, or cleanup first?

Fast is useful. Wrong is expensive.
