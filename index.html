Quick Routing Reminder

Do not route by panic.

Before you pitch a funding product, check the borrower’s use of funds, repayment source, urgency, documents, existing debt, and cash-flow pressure. Fast is useful. Wrong is expensive.

  • What is the money actually for?
  • How will repayment fit the business cash flow?
  • Is this urgent, or just underprepared?
  • Is the borrower ready for funding, or do they need cleanup first?

This resource is for educational and broker workflow support only. It does not guarantee funding approval, pricing, repayment terms, lender eligibility, credit outcomes, or funding timelines. Eligibility and terms vary by provider and borrower profile.
